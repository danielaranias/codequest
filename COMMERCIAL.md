# Using CodeQuest commercially

**Free, no permission needed:**

- Use CodeQuest on any code, including at work and on private company repos.
- Change it, fork it, share your changes.
- Use it in teaching, talks, videos and courses you give yourself.

**Needs a paid commercial licence:**

- Selling CodeQuest, or a fork of it.
- Selling a product or hosted service whose value comes mostly from CodeQuest
  (for example "CodeQuest as a service", or bundling it into a paid developer tool).
- Paid consulting or support that is mainly about CodeQuest.

To ask for a licence, open an issue titled **"Commercial licence"** on this repository
(you do not need to share details in public; we will move to a private channel).
